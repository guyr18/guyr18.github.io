Selected GTRI Work

Research Scientist I · Real-Time Systems · Electronic Warfare · Radar

At GTRI, I worked on real-time C++/Python services and distributed software for hardware-in-the-loop electronic-warfare testbeds and fielded radar systems. My work included low-latency telemetry and control, hardware abstraction, heterogeneous RF interfaces, system integration, and end-to-end delivery for multi-lab test events.

ENGINEERING

Systems Work

Real-Time Distributed Backends

Designed and hardened high-throughput, low-latency services for control and telemetry, including multithreaded C++, ZeroMQ/gRPC interfaces, and containerized deployments under strict timing constraints.

Hardware-in-the-Loop EW Testbeds

Built software supporting hardware-in-the-loop electronic-warfare experimentation, integrating mission software with RF front ends, record/playback systems, compute nodes, and signal-processing chains.

Open Hardware Interfaces

Developed hardware-abstraction layers and protobuf-based interfaces that decoupled mission logic from heterogeneous RF and compute hardware.

Technical Leadership & Integration

Led telemetry and simulation efforts, coordinated integration with DSP/RF teams, supported design reviews, and owned end-to-end delivery for multi-lab Angry Kitten experiments.
